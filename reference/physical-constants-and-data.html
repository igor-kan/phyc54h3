Conversion Factors

Energy

  • \(1 \text{ eV} = 1.602176634 \times 10^{-19} \text{ J}\)
  • \(1 \text{ erg} = 10^{-7} \text{ J}\)
  • \(1 \text{ calorie} = 4.184 \text{ J}\)

Distance

  • \(1 \text{ Angstrom (\AA)} = 10^{-10} \text{ m}\)
  • \(1 \text{ Astronomical Unit (AU)} = 1.495978707 \times 10^{11} \text{ m}\)
  • \(1 \text{ Light Year (ly)} = 9.4607 \times 10^{15} \text{ m}\)
  • \(1 \text{ Parsec (pc)} = 3.0857 \times 10^{16} \text{ m} = 3.26 \text{ ly}\)

Pressure

  • \(1 \text{ Atmosphere (atm)} = 101,325 \text{ Pa}\)
  • \(1 \text{ bar} = 100,000 \text{ Pa}\)
  • \(1 \text{ Torr (mmHg)} = 133.322 \text{ Pa}\)

# Conversion Factors

## Energy
*   $1 \text{ eV} = 1.602176634 \times 10^{-19} \text{ J}$
*   $1 \text{ erg} = 10^{-7} \text{ J}$
*   $1 \text{ calorie} = 4.184 \text{ J}$

## Distance
*   $1 \text{ Angstrom (\AA)} = 10^{-10} \text{ m}$
*   $1 \text{ Astronomical Unit (AU)} = 1.495978707 \times 10^{11} \text{ m}$
*   $1 \text{ Light Year (ly)} = 9.4607 \times 10^{15} \text{ m}$
*   $1 \text{ Parsec (pc)} = 3.0857 \times 10^{16} \text{ m} = 3.26 \text{ ly}$

## Pressure
*   $1 \text{ Atmosphere (atm)} = 101,325 \text{ Pa}$
*   $1 \text{ bar} = 100,000 \text{ Pa}$
*   $1 \text{ Torr (mmHg)} = 133.322 \text{ Pa}$
